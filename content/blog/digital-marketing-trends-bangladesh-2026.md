## The unfashionable order of work

The feeds are loud. The order that still holds is quieter.

First, can a stranger tell what you sell, what it costs, and how to get it, without a phone call to your cousin? If the answer is no, a trend will not fix it. A clear offer on the page or the site comes before any boost.

Second, can you reply? Messaging campaigns fail in the gap between the notification and the answer. No format change repairs a shop that sees the message at 11pm and replies on Thursday.

Third, only then is it worth paying Meta for more of those conversations, calls or visits. The [Facebook ad cost note](/blog/facebook-ad-cost-bangladesh) is the budget half of that decision. The ranges there disagree on purpose. Use them as a warning, then use your own account.

## Short video, without the magic multiple

Reels and short video are where a lot of scrolling happens. Meta’s ad tools let you place ads there. That is a product fact, visible in Ads Manager, not a statistic we measured across Bangladesh. We do not publish a universal engagement multiple for short video, because the honest studies are tied to a platform, a date and a sample, and a recycled “10x” line usually is not.

What you can do: film the product in the room where it is sold. Show the price. Show the area you deliver to, or say collection only. Use the real voice of the person who will answer WhatsApp. A studio template with a stock skyline does not become local because you added a district name in the caption.

If you boost that video, know you are using the simple tool. [Meta’s explanation of boosted posts](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/317083072148603) still applies: fewer options than a standard ad. When the video is part of a plan with a radius and a result, build it in Ads Manager. The [takeaway comparison](/blog/meta-ads-vs-boosted-posts-uk-takeaways) is written for the UK, and the tool split is the same here.

## AI, in the size a small team can use

AI-written captions and AI images are easy to buy. They are useful as a draft. They are a poor face for a clinic, a kitchen or a school if nobody checks them. Customers in Bangladesh notice a fake address and a fake price faster than a think-piece does.

Use AI for the chores: resizing, first-pass captions, sorting messages into “price”, “location” and “complaint”. Do not use it to invent reviews or a user count. Do not use it to promise a result Ads Manager has not reported.

A custom chat tool on your site is a different project from a caption assistant. Marketivity builds that kind of thing as an [AI web app](/services/ai-web-apps) when the workflow is real: a document helper, a logged-in product, a demo with a limit on usage. Model fees are billed on the client’s own account. They are not folded into a slogan about “AI marketing”. If you only need a WhatsApp button, you do not need that build.

## Websites people can still open

A Facebook page is enough while every order is a conversation you enjoy having. It gets fragile when the page rules change, the admin leaves, or a customer wants a link they can trust. A site with a current price, a map and a form is the boring trend that keeps paying.

The cost conversation is in [Website development in Rajshahi](/blog/website-development-rajshahi). Short version: published Bangladesh bands are wide, Marketivity’s set taka fees are ৳18,000, ৳40,000 and from ৳80,000 for the three website tiers, and the [pricing page](/pricing) shows the dollar and pound cards beside that schedule. Hosting is not a surprise you should discover in month two. Put the renewal in the brief.

Local pages should say the city in ordinary language. Stuffing “Rajshahi” into every heading reads like a template. Say where you are, which areas you serve, and what you do not do. The [local listing guide](/blog/seo-local-business-rajshahi) covers the Google profile that should match the site.

## Paid social, with the fee on its own line

The pattern we see in briefs is a single monthly number that mixes the manager and the media. Split them.

Media spend goes to Meta from an ad account in your name. You can export it. The management fee is what you pay for the person who builds the campaign, reads it and writes the note. Marketivity’s Meta Ads tiers are ৳8,000, ৳15,000 and from ৳25,000 a month, with the ad budget on top. That is a set fee, not a percentage of a hoped-for return.

Name the result before the first taka of spend. A message, a call, a visit, or a purchase your shop system can see. Client-reported, and only as an average of campaigns where the client counted: about 17–20 sales per messaging campaign. It is not a 2026 trend line and not a quote.

Meta’s [learning phase](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700) still says about 50 results in a week after a big edit. Small accounts often miss that bar. The trend is not “spend more until the column turns green”. The trend is “edit less, and do not call a learning limit a moral failure”.

## What we are not calling a trend

We are not calling a particular hashtag a strategy. We are not ranking districts by CPM. We are not telling you to target an ethnic group. Meta does not offer that, and a small business does not need it. Location, language of the creative, and people who already talked to you are enough to start.

We are also not telling every Rajshahi shop to open a Shopify store this quarter. If you sell three products to people who already know you, a page and a simple site can be the whole plan. If you sell to people who will never visit, look at the [Shopify budget note](/blog/shopify-meta-ads-budget) before you copy a daily dollar amount from a US thread.

## A 2026 setup that fits on one page

- Offer, price, area, hours.
- One place the customer can act: WhatsApp, a form, or a checkout.
- Accounts in your name.
- If you advertise, one objective and a fee that is not disguised as spend.
- A monthly export, read by a person, with the client’s own sales count kept separate when it exists.

That is less exciting than a trend carousel. It is the work. If you want it scoped, start with [contact](/contact) or the [service list](/services). Bring the offer. Leave the invented national statistics out of the message. We will not add them back in to make the proposal feel larger.
