## What “a website” means in this price conversation

A Rajshahi shop that needs a phone number, a map and a WhatsApp button is not buying the same thing as a catalogue with payments. Write the pages down before you ask for a quote. Home, about, services, one proof page and contact is five pages. A menu, a booking form and an English and Bangla version is already a larger brief.

## Questions to ask before you pay

Send these in writing. A serious studio will answer them without a slide deck.

### Who owns the site after launch?

You should receive the repository, the design files you paid for, and accounts in your name: domain, hosting, analytics, Search Console. If the studio’s name stays on the hosting bill and you cannot export the site, you are renting a page.

### What does the fee include, in pages?

“Up to five pages” means five URLs, not five scrolling sections on one URL, unless they say so. Ask whether Bangla copy, product photography and a logo are inside the fee or billed later.

### How many revision rounds, and what is a round?

Two rounds means two passes on the agreed screens after you have seen them. It does not mean unlimited new pages. Ask what happens if you change the offer halfway through.

### Will it work on a mid-range Android phone?

Most of your customers are not on the laptop used in the demo. Ask them to show a real handset, not only a narrowed browser window. Ask who compresses images. A beautiful desktop that takes ten seconds to open on a phone will not get the WhatsApp tap.

### What do they mean by SEO?

Basic on-page SEO is titles, descriptions, headings and a page that states where you are. It is not a promise of page-one rankings. Nobody honest sells “number one in Rajshahi” as a line item. If you also need the Google listing, that is a separate job from the design. The [local listing notes](/blog/seo-local-business-rajshahi) sit next to this one on purpose.

### What happens in month two?

Domain renewal, hosting and small edits are ongoing. Softwix puts maintenance in a monthly band of their own. Marketivity’s care extras are separate from the build. If the quote is silent, budget for a renewal you control.

## A Rajshahi brief you can copy

Write one page before the call.

- The offer in one sentence, and who it is for.
- The neighbourhood and how people reach you.
- The action you want: call, WhatsApp, form, or a visit.
- Pages you need in the first version, and pages that can wait.
- Languages: Bangla, English, or both.
- Photos you already have, and photos you do not.
- The date you actually need it, and what “done” means.

That brief is how you compare two quotes. Without it, the cheaper PDF is often the one that left out the form, the map and the handover.

## After you choose a studio

Pay a deposit you can describe, against a written list of pages, not against a vibe. Keep the rest of the fee for handover, when you can log in and the form sends a message you received. Ask for a staging link before anything is public. Click every button on your phone. Read the Bangla if you asked for Bangla. A cousin who speaks both languages will see machine-translated menus faster than you will.

Keep a folder with the domain login, the hosting login, the repository and the analytics property. If the studio is kind, they will send that folder without a chase. If they need three reminders, believe that pattern. It is the same pattern you will get when something breaks on a Friday.

Rajshahi businesses often start from a Facebook page that already sells. The site should not fight that page. Use the same offer, the same phone and the same photos, then let the site do what the page cannot: a stable link, a map, and a page you can send to a bank or a landlord. When the page and the site disagree on price, fix them the same day. Customers do not care which one was “the latest design”.

If you are also about to advertise, do not launch ads at a half-built contact form. The [ad cost guide](/blog/facebook-ad-cost-bangladesh) assumes a place for the click to land. Build that place first, even if it is only the Starter tier.
