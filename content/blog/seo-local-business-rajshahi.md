## What local search is asking

Someone types a service and a place, or they search the business name because a friend said it. Google may show a map pack, a website, or both. You do not control the mix. You control whether your listing is complete and whether the site agrees with it.

[Google’s Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038063) is the place to start for how profiles work and how you manage one. Read Google’s page when the interface moves. Advice that depends on a button name from 2022 will strand you. The habits below do not depend on a button name.

## The profile

Claim the profile for the real location. If you serve customers at a counter in Rajshahi, the pin should be that counter, not a cousin’s house in another city. If you visit clients and have no shopfront, use the service-area options Google offers instead of pretending there is a showroom.

Then check the fields a customer uses.

- Hours, including Friday and any break the kitchen actually takes.
- The phone number that is answered.
- The category that matches the work. A clothing shop listed as a marketing agency will collect the wrong clicks.
- Photos of the real place, the real product, the real person who greets people. A stock skyline of a different country is not local SEO. It is a reason to distrust you.
- The website link, once you have a site. Until then, do not send people to a dead Facebook tab if a current page exists.

If the hours on the glass door and the hours on the profile differ, fix the profile the same day. Map results that send someone to a locked shutter are how a good product gets a bad review.

## One name, one phone, one address

Directories and social bios copy each other badly. Pick the spelling of the business name and keep it. Pick the phone number that rings. Pick the address you want a rickshaw to use.

Put that trio on:

- the Google profile
- the website footer
- the Facebook page about section
- any Bangladesh directory you choose to join and can log in to later

If an old listing has a retired number, update it or close it. Leaving three phones in the wild means some customers call a stranger. You will not see those calls in analytics, which is why owners swear “the internet doesn’t work” while the old SIM is in a drawer.

## The page on your site

Create one page that sounds like a person in the shop.

Say what you sell. Say which parts of Rajshahi you serve, or that people collect from you. Mention a landmark only if it helps someone find the door. Mention local events only when you are actually part of them. A paragraph about a festival you did not join, written to catch a search, is thin. Customers can tell.

Titles and headings should include the place when the place is the point. “Tailoring in Rajshahi” is a reasonable title if you are a tailor in Rajshahi. “Best tailor Rajshahi near me cheap open now” is a heading written for a robot. Google’s own public guidance has spent years telling site owners to write for people. Follow that even when a freelancer offers a keyword list that reads like a ransom note.

Pictures need ordinary descriptions. “Blue panjabi on a hanger in the shop” is alt text. “Best panjabi Rajshahi” stuffed into an image name is not a caption a screen-reader user can use.

## Reviews, without the grey market

Ask people who bought. Ask once. Make it easy: a short link, a QR code on the receipt, a sentence at the counter. Do not offer money for stars. Do not post reviews from staff under other names. Those patterns get listings into trouble, and they read as false to the next customer.

Reply to the review you wish you had not received. A calm correction of a wrong fact is enough. An argument is a second piece of content, and it ranks in the only way that matters: the next person sees you fighting.

You cannot require every happy customer to post. A handful of specific reviews (“the grey three-piece was ready on Thursday”) is more useful than fifty identical “great service” lines.

## What not to buy

Do not buy a guarantee of the map pack. The pack moves. A new competitor can open across the road. Google can change the layout. A studio that sells “top 3 in 30 days” is selling a result they do not control.

Do not buy a pile of exact-match blog posts that exist only to repeat Rajshahi. One clear service page beats ten watery ones. If you later want notes on ads, the [Bangladesh ad cost guide](/blog/facebook-ad-cost-bangladesh) and the [2026 marketing note](/blog/digital-marketing-trends-bangladesh-2026) are about paid and organic choices without a fake national user count.

Do not hide the address if customers visit you, and do not publish a home address you do not want on the internet if they do not. Service-area businesses should use the tools Google provides for that case rather than a fake shopfront.

4. Read the last five reviews and answer any you ignored.
